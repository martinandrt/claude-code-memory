## Retention policy

- **feedback/** — never auto-archived (durable lessons keep their value); `memory-check.py`
  emits a soft warning past a count cap, as a nudge to consolidate.
- **projects/** — review when `last_edited` is older than the staleness threshold; a finished
  project moves to an archive or gets deleted.
- **orphans** — a file nothing links to *and* older than the threshold is an archive candidate;
  review by hand.

*The numbers and thresholds live in `scripts/memory-check.py` — adjust them to your cadence.*
